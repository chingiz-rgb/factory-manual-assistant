# Lockout/Tagout and Shop Safety Procedure

> Sample procedure written for the Factory Manual Assistant demo. Not a real company's safety rule. Always follow your own site's procedures.

## 1. When Lockout/Tagout Is Required

Lockout/tagout (LOTO) is required before any work where a person's body could enter a danger zone of a machine, for example:

- clearing a jam inside the machine
- replacing a belt, chuck or guard
- cleaning inside the machine enclosure
- any electrical or hydraulic repair

Pressing EMERGENCY STOP is **not** a lockout. Emergency stop can be reset by another person.

## 2. Lockout Steps

1. Tell the operators and the team leader that you are going to lock out the machine.
2. Stop the machine with the normal stop button.
3. Turn the main power switch to OFF.
4. Put your personal red padlock on the main switch. Each worker uses their own lock and keeps their own key.
5. Attach a "DANGER – DO NOT OPERATE" tag with your name, date and the reason.
6. Release stored energy: wait until the spindle has completely stopped, and open the hydraulic release valve until the pressure gauge shows 0 MPa.
7. Try to start the machine to verify it does not start. Then return the start switch to OFF.

## 3. Removing the Lockout

1. Check that all tools are removed and all guards are reinstalled.
2. Make sure everyone is clear of the machine.
3. Only the person who put on the lock may remove it.
4. Tell the operators that the machine is ready.

If the person who put on the lock has left the site, only the section manager may remove it, after contacting that person.

## 4. Personal Protective Equipment

| Area | Required PPE |
|---|---|
| Machining area | Safety glasses, safety shoes |
| Grinding area | Safety glasses, face shield, safety shoes, dust mask |
| Coolant handling | Safety glasses, nitrile gloves |
| Forklift area | Safety shoes, high-visibility vest |

Gloves must not be worn when operating lathes, drilling machines or other machines with rotating parts, because they can be caught and pull the hand in.

## 5. Injuries and Near Misses

1. Stop the machine and make the area safe.
2. Give first aid. The first aid kit is next to the fire extinguisher at each line.
3. Report every injury and every near miss to the team leader on the same day, even if nobody was hurt.
