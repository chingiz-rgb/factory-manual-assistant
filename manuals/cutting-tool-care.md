# Cutting Tool Care and Inspection Guide

> Sample guide written for the Factory Manual Assistant demo. Values are examples only.

## 1. Daily Inspection

Before using a cutting tool, look at the cutting edge under the inspection lamp. Do not use the tool if you see any of the following:

- chipping larger than 0.2 mm on the cutting edge
- a crack in the insert or the tool body
- built-up edge that cannot be removed with a brass brush
- discoloration (blue or purple) on a carbide insert, which shows overheating

## 2. When to Replace a Carbide Insert

Replace or index the insert to a new corner when any one of these is true:

1. Flank wear (VB) reaches 0.3 mm for finishing or 0.5 mm for roughing.
2. The surface roughness of the part becomes worse than Ra 3.2 µm on a finishing pass.
3. Chatter or a new noise appears that did not happen with a fresh edge.
4. The tool life count on the machine reaches the limit set in the tool data sheet.

Never turn an insert to a new corner while the spindle is rotating. Press EMERGENCY STOP first.

## 3. Drills and End Mills

Solid carbide drills may be reground up to 3 times. After regrinding, the overall length must be at least 90% of the original length, otherwise the drill is scrapped. Put scrapped tools in the red "SCRAP CARBIDE" box, not in the general waste bin. Carbide is recycled.

## 4. Storage

1. Store inserts in their original cases. Do not put loose inserts together in a box, because the edges chip when they touch.
2. Keep drills and end mills in the tool rack with the cutting end protected by a plastic cap.
3. Every tool must have a label with its tool number. Tools without a label are sent to the tool crib for checking.

## 5. Tool Records

When a tool is taken out of the tool crib, scan its barcode at the tool station. When it is returned, scan it again and enter the condition: GOOD, REGRIND or SCRAP. This record is used to plan tool purchases, so do not skip the scan.
